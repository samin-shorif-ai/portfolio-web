03 — ABOUT

I turn messy, manual work into systems that people rely on every day.

SAMIN SHORIF — IRL

I start with the business, not the code: where is it losing time or money, and what would fixing that be worth? Then I design, build and ship the fix, and stay with it until people rely on it.

I'm AI-native: AI is part of every step of how I work — research, prototyping, writing and reviewing code — so an idea becomes a working product fast. Deciding what's worth building is still a human job, and it's the part I care about most.
