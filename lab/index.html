for the hero.

Each one runs behind the real hero text, on phones too. Tap “Text” in the top bar to hide the text and see the effect on its own. None of these are on the live site yet.

  1. 01Interfering waves↗ Today's wave plus real wave physics. Tap to drop a wave source; two sources at the same frequency make an interference pattern. Move the mouse or drag a finger to leave a wake.
  2. 02Flowing particles↗ Lime particles ride a swirling current. Your cursor or finger is a rock in the stream: the flow parts in front of it and closes behind it.
  3. 03Name from particles↗ The particles assemble into SAMIN SHORIF, get pushed by your cursor or finger, and break apart as you scroll down — then rebuild when you scroll back up.
